Why We Exist

Errands, Done Right — In A City That Never Slows Down

Anyone who lives in Lagos knows the drill: a two-kilometre trip swallows an hour on Third Mainland, dispatch riders vanish mid-delivery, and "I'm five minutes away" can mean anything. We started Za.allyErrands in Lagos, in 2022, after one too many important packages went missing and one too many afternoons lost to gridlock. The city moves on hustle — but hustle without reliable logistics is just stress.

So we built the network we wished existed: a bench of background-checked runners who know Lagos' streets, connected to real-time tracking and an API-first dispatch layer that treats every errand — a document drop in Ikeja, groceries to Lekki, a queue held at the bank — as a promise worth keeping. No ghosting. No guesswork. Just your errand, handled.

How We Started

From One Errand To A Network

2022

A frustration on the Mainland

It began with a handful of friends in Yaba running errands for busy neighbours and small shops — and realising the demand was everywhere. The idea: what if reliable dispatch in Lagos was a service you could actually count on?

2023

Runners, verified and on the map

We formalised the runner network with proper vetting, onboarding, and live tracking. Word spread across Surulere, Ikeja, and Lekki as customers came back — because their packages actually arrived, on time.

2024

Dispatch goes API-first

Lagos businesses wanted to plug delivery straight into their own apps and storefronts. We opened up our dispatch layer, turning Za.allyErrands into infrastructure other Lagos brands could build on.

Today

15+ zones and moving forward

Thousands of errands a month, a growing bench of trusted runners, and one mission that hasn't changed since Yaba: give Lagos its time back.
